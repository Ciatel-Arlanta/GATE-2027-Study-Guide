# General Aptitude checkpoint

Try without notes; solutions are folded.

**Q1 (NAT).** A value changes from 80 to 92. Find the percentage increase.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> **15%.** Increase 12; $12/80\cdot100=15$.</details>

**Q2 (NAT).** A can do a job in 10 days and B in 15. Time together?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Rate $1/10+1/15=1/6$, so **6 days**.</details>

**Q3 (MCQ).** Choose 2 from 6: (A) 12 (B) 15 (C) 30 (D) 36.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> **B.** $\binom62=15$.</details>

**Q4.** If $P\to Q$ is true and Q is false, what can be concluded?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $P$ is false; this is the contrapositive.</details>

**Q5 (NAT).** Rotate $(1,4)$ 90° clockwise around the origin.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $(4,-1)$, since clockwise maps $(x,y)\to(y,-x)$.</details>

**Q6.** Sales are 120 units in year 1 and 150 in year 2. Find relative increase.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> **25%.** Increase 30 over original 120.</details>

**Q7.** “All A are B; no B are C.” What follows about A and C?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> No A is C: every A lies in B, which is disjoint from C.</details>

**Q8.** Two equal trips are driven at 30 km/h and 60 km/h. Find average speed.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> **40 km/h.** For equal distances, $2/(1/30+1/60)=40$.</details>
