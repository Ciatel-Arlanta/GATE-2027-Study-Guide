# Exam strategy

## Paper pattern (CS and DA)

Item | Detail
--- | ---
Duration | 3 hours, computer-based, on-screen virtual calculator (practise with it — no physical calculator)
Questions / marks | 65 questions, 100 marks
General Aptitude | 10 questions, 15 marks (5 × 1-mark + 5 × 2-mark), common to every paper
Subject | 55 questions, 85 marks. In CS, roughly 13 of these are Engineering Mathematics.
Question types | **MCQ** (one correct option) · **MSQ** (one or more correct options) · **NAT** (type in a number)
Negative marking | MCQ only: −1/3 for a wrong 1-mark answer, −2/3 for a wrong 2-mark answer
No negative marking | MSQ and NAT. MSQ has **no partial credit**: you must select exactly the correct set.
Two papers | CS + DA is a permitted two-paper combination; the papers are held in different sessions

Always confirm the current year's details in the official GATE 2027 information brochure. The organising institute is IIT Madras.

## The PYQ method

Your PYQ banks are in the parent folder:

- CS: [GATE_PYQ_PART_1](<../PYQs Qbs/GATE_PYQ_PART_1.pdf>), [PART_2](<../PYQs Qbs/GATE_PYQ_PART_2.pdf>), [PART_3](<../PYQs Qbs/GATE_PYQ_PART_3.pdf>)
- DA: [GATE_DA_PYQ](<../PYQs Qbs/GATE_DA_PYQ.pdf>). The DA paper only started in 2024, so also solve CS PYQs on the shared subjects (probability, linear algebra, DS/algorithms, DBMS).

Per topic:

1. Study the chapter; do its Practice questions.
2. Solve **every** PYQ on that topic, timed: about 1.5 min for a 1-mark question and 3 min for a 2-mark question.
3. For each wrong answer, write one line in a mistakes log: *topic · what I did · the correct idea*. Most mistakes match an existing **GATE traps** entry. If yours does not, add it to that chapter's traps list.
4. Re-solve the wrong ones after a week.

Per subject: take the section `CHECKPOINT.md`, then a full-subject PYQ set under time.

## Exam-day tactics

- **Three passes.** Pass 1 (≈ 60 min): every question you can do in under 2 minutes, GA first. Pass 2 (≈ 80 min): the 2-mark calculation questions you know how to do. Pass 3: everything else, then review.
- **MCQ guessing.** With −1/3 or −2/3 penalties, guess only if you can eliminate at least two options.
- **MSQ.** Check every option independently, as if each were a separate true/false question. One wrong tick loses the whole question, so select an option only when you are sure of it.
- **NAT.** Read the rounding instruction ("up to 2 decimal places", "integer"). Keep full precision until the last step. Check units: bits vs bytes, KB = 2¹⁰ or 10³ (the question says which), ms vs µs.
- **Trace, don't simulate in your head.** For code, scheduling, page replacement, cache, DP and parsing questions, draw the table on the rough sheet. The tables in these notes show the exact format to use.
- **GA is the cheapest 15 marks.** Do not leave it for the end.

## The last 30 days

1. Days 30–15: one full mock every 2–3 days. Analyse each mock for twice as long as you took to write it.
2. Days 15–3: only [cheat sheets](CHEATSHEETS.md), GATE traps lists, your mistakes log, and re-solving PYQs you got wrong.
3. Days 2–1: cheat sheets once, sleep properly, no new topics.
