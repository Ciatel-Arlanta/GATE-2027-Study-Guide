# Probability and Statistics checkpoint

**Q1.** If $P(A)=0.4,P(B)=0.5,P(A\cap B)=0.2$, find $P(A\cup B)$.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $0.4+0.5-0.2=0.7$.</details>

**Q2.** If A and B are independent with probabilities 0.3 and 0.2, find probability both occur.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $0.3\cdot0.2=0.06$.</details>

**Q3.** A disease affects 1% of a population. A test is 90% sensitive and has 5% false positive rate. Find disease probability after a positive result.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Bayes: $0.9(0.01)/[0.9(0.01)+0.05(0.99)]=0.009/0.0585\approx0.154$ (15.4%).</details>

**Q4 (NAT).** $X$ is Bernoulli with success probability 0.3. Find variance.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $p(1-p)=0.21$.</details>

**Q5.** $X\sim Bin(10,0.2)$. Find mean and variance.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Mean 2; variance $10(0.2)(0.8)=1.6$.</details>

**Q6.** $X\sim Exp(4)$ with rate 4. Find $P(X>0.5)$.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $e^{-4(0.5)}=e^{-2}$.</details>

**Q7.** $X\sim N(20,3^2)$. Find z-score for x=14.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $(14-20)/3=-2$.</details>

**Q8.** If $\sigma=15,n=25$, find standard error of the sample mean.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $15/5=3$.</details>

**Q9.** What does a p-value measure?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Under $H_0$, probability of an outcome at least as extreme as observed.</details>
