# Calculus checkpoint

**Q1.** Differentiate $x^3\sin x$.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $3x^2\sin x+x^3\cos x$ by product rule.</details>

**Q2.** Find derivative of $e^{2x}$.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $2e^{2x}$ by chain rule.</details>

**Q3.** Find critical points of $f(x)=x^2-4x+1$ and classify.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $f'=2x-4=0$ at x=2; $f''=2>0$, local/global minimum; value −3.</details>

**Q4.** Find maximum of $f(x)=x$ on $[0,1]$.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> 1, at endpoint x=1. Endpoints must be checked.</details>

**Q5.** Evaluate $\int_0^1 3x^2dx$.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $[x^3]_0^1=1$.</details>

**Q6.** State the mean value theorem's key conclusion.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Some interior derivative equals the secant slope between the endpoints.</details>

**Q7.** First three Taylor terms for $e^x$ around 0.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $1+x+x^2/2$.</details>

**Q8.** Why is $\int_{-1}^1x^3dx=0$?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> The integrand is odd over a symmetric interval.</details>
