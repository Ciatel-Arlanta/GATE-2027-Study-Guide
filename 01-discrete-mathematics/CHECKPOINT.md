# Discrete Mathematics checkpoint

**Q1 (MCQ).** $P\to Q$ is false when: (A) P,Q true (B) P true,Q false (C) P false,Q true (D) both false.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> **B.** Implication only fails when its premise holds and conclusion fails.</details>

**Q2.** Negate “every vertex has degree at least one.”
<details><summary>Answer</summary> There exists a vertex with degree zero.</details>

**Q3.** On integers, $aRb$ iff $a-b$ is divisible by 5. Name the relation's classes.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Residue classes modulo 5: numbers congruent to 0, 1, 2, 3, or 4 mod 5.</details>

**Q4 (NAT).** Number of 3-element subsets of a 7-element set?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $\binom73=35$.</details>

**Q5.** An undirected graph has degree sequence 1, 2, 2, 3. How many edges?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Degree sum 8, so 4 edges.</details>

**Q6.** A tree has 20 vertices. Number of edges?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> 19.</details>

**Q7.** Is a 7-cycle bipartite?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> No. Odd cycles are not bipartite; it needs 3 colours.</details>

**Q8.** Solve $T(n)=2T(n/2)+n$ asymptotically.
<details><summary>Answer</summary> $\Theta(n\log n)$; each of $\log n$ levels does linear total work.</details>

**Q9.** Is divisibility on positive integers a total order?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> No; for instance 2 and 3 are incomparable.</details>

**Q10.** For $A=\{a,b\}$, meet and join of $\{a\}$ and $\{b\}$ in the subset lattice?
<details><summary>Answer</summary> Meet $\emptyset$, join $\{a,b\}$.</details>
